Here's the thing

None of that is about Christmas.

It's years of hurt you've had to swallow. The comment about your weight that still stings. The things you never got to say. The memories you'd rather forget.

They stack up over Christmas like giant Jenga, and you're the one trying to stop it all falling down while spinning plates with a broom up your bum.

It's not a YOU problem. It's emotional baggage. And it can be cleared.
