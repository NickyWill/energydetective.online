Cookie Notice

Last updated: September 2026

This Cookie Notice explains how Nicky Williams and Energy Detective use cookies and similar technologies on this website. By continuing to browse or use this site, you agree to the use of cookies as described below.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use the site.

Cookies do not give us access to your computer or personal information beyond what you choose to share.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the website works correctly
  • Improve site performance
  • Analyse website traffic and usage
  • Remember your preferences
  • Support secure payment and login functions
  • Enhance your overall user experience

Some cookies are essential for the website to function.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These are required for core website functions such as:

  • Security
  • Navigation
  • Accessing protected areas of the site

Without these cookies, the website cannot operate properly.

Analytics and Performance Cookies

We may use analytics tools to understand:

  • Which pages are visited
  • How long visitors stay
  • How the website is used

This helps us improve the site and user experience.

Marketing and Tracking Cookies

If used, these cookies help us:

  • Track the effectiveness of ads
  • Understand visitor interests
  • Show relevant content or marketing

These may come from trusted third parties such as Meta or email and messaging platforms.

Functionality Cookies

These remember your preferences, such as:

  • Language settings
  • Display choices
  • Previously completed forms

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties we work with, such as:

  • Flodesk (email and sign-up forms)
  • ManyChat (Instagram messaging and automated replies)
  • ThriveCart (checkout and payments)
  • Stripe or PayPal (secure payments)
  • Analytics providers
  • Embedded content (videos or external tools)

These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can choose to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Block non-essential cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Set your browser to notify you when cookies are used

Your browser settings and device controls allow you to manage cookie preferences at any time.

Please note that disabling some cookies may affect the functionality or performance of the website.

This Cookie Notice may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
