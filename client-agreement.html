02. What Energy Work Is — and Is Not

The Deep Release Method™ is a complementary, energy-based approach. It is not a form of medical, psychological, or psychiatric treatment.

Energy work does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical or psychological condition. Nothing provided by Energy Detective constitutes medical advice, psychological advice, or a clinical recommendation of any kind.

Energy work is intended to support emotional clarity, energetic balance, and personal wellbeing. It works alongside — and does not replace — professional medical or mental health care.

Results vary between individuals and cannot be predicted or guaranteed. Your outcomes will depend on factors including personal history, emotional readiness, and individual energy patterns.

03. Scope of Work and What Falls Outside It

Energy Detective works within a defined scope. The following fall outside that scope and cannot be addressed within any session or programme:

Outside scope — Energy Detective does not provide:

  • Crisis support or mental health intervention
  • Diagnosis or treatment of any medical or psychological condition
  • Advice on psychiatric medication, dosage, or withdrawal
  • Substitute care for clients under active psychiatric treatment
  • Emergency or urgent care of any kind
  • Therapeutic counselling or psychotherapy
  • Support for acute suicidal ideation, self-harm, or psychotic episodes

If any of the above applies to your situation, you are encouraged to seek support from a qualified medical or mental health professional. Energy Detective is not the right fit for clients whose primary need is clinical mental health care.

Nicky reserves the right to decline or discontinue work with any client where the session content moves into territory outside this scope — without refund where sessions have already been delivered.

04. Mental Health and Medication — Your Responsibility to Disclose

Energy work can surface emotions, memories, and patterns that have been held below conscious awareness. For most people, this is a normal and manageable part of the process.

However, if you are currently experiencing — or have a history of — significant mental health challenges, it is important that this is disclosed before work begins.

You agree to inform Energy Detective if you are currently under the care of a psychiatrist or mental health team, taking prescribed psychiatric medication, experiencing active mental health episodes, or have been hospitalised for psychiatric reasons within the past two years.

This information is held in confidence and is used solely to ensure the work is appropriate for your situation.

Failure to disclose this information does not transfer responsibility to Energy Detective. You remain fully responsible for the information you provide at intake and throughout the working relationship.

Energy Detective does not provide advice on psychiatric medication under any circumstances. If you have questions about your medication, please consult your prescribing doctor.

08. Processing and After-Effects

Energy work can sometimes result in a period of processing after a session. This may include temporary emotional sensitivity, tiredness, vivid dreams, or the surfacing of old feelings. This is a normal part of energetic release and typically passes within 24–72 hours.

If you experience anything that concerns you following a session, please contact hello@energydetective.online. If you experience a mental health crisis at any time, please contact your GP, NHS 111, or emergency services immediately.
