Frequently Asked Questions

How does a distance session work if we're not on a call?

All sessions are done remotely. There is no call, Zoom or appointment time. With your permission, I connect to your energy field, identify what needs to be released and clear it. You simply go about your day and receive your personalised video once the session is complete.

Do I need to talk about or relive my past?

No. I don't work at the level of the story. I work at the level of the signal. You don't need to explain anything or revisit anything painful.

What's the difference between Block Release and Rewire?

A Block Release Session is one sitting, aimed at whatever's most active for you right now. Rewire is four sessions over four weeks, for working through more than one layer as it surfaces. Both are entirely remote, using the Deep Release Method™, with no call needed at all.

Which option is right for me?

If there's one specific thing on your mind, start with a Block Release Session. If a session clears something and you feel there's more underneath, Rewire is your next step. Still not sure? Email me at hello@energydetective.online and I'll help you decide.

Do I need to believe in energy work for it to work?

No. Plenty of my clients started out as sceptics. You don't need to believe in it, you just need to be willing to let it happen. Your belief is not required for the clearing to work.

When will I notice changes?

Some shifts are immediate. Others unfold over days or weeks as your subconscious and nervous system settle into what's been cleared. Your personalised video will tell you what to watch for.

How do I get started?

Choose the option that feels right for where you are right now and follow the link. If you're unsure, read what clients have said on the testimonials page or send a message to hello@energydetective.online.
