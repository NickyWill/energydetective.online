Why I do this work

I've walked the same path as you.

None of it resolved at the level I was trying to heal at: the GPs, the consultants, the standard alternative therapies. After spending too long trying to fix it with solutions that just don't stick, I realised I was looking in the wrong place.

Turns out the answer wasn't more information, more self development, more trauma healing and tears. It wasn't something to understand, or relive, or feel my way through. It just needed clearing.

Now I help women clear the outdated data running their subconscious, so the woman looking back in the mirror finally feels like her again.

How this work actually works

I don't need your story. I need a few breadcrumbs, that's all.

One or two sentences. Your name. Your date of birth. That's it.

From there I connect the dots. I go straight into your internal GPS (your subconscious), and find where the old, outdated data is still blocking your health, wealth, relationships or business.

I don't start with the symptom and guess. I follow the signal back to where it started. The specific moment. The specific reason it got programmed in. The specific reason it's still running, on a loop, long after it stopped being true.

Then I clear it.

You don't have to relive your history. You don't have to sit with your trauma and cry it out with me. You don't have to spend an hour in your feelings or write anything down. You don't have to get on a Zoom call.

You go about your day. I do the rest.
